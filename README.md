### About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Crete (CSD UoC) with a focus on Android development and systems programming. I build native Android applications in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and an MVVM architecture, and I work comfortably in C, C++, Python and Lua for coursework and personal projects.

I care about clean, maintainable code, thoughtful user experience and shipping complete, well-documented projects.
